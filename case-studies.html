Dhaka Bank PLC

2021 – Present

Full-stack Engineer

Enterprise Banking Workflow Platform

One reusable workflow engine, extended into nine-plus production banking systems — instead of building each from scratch.

Problem

Dhaka Bank needed to digitize a series of manual, paper-and-spreadsheet-driven processes — bank guarantee issuance, e-lending approvals, card operations, credit limit management, tax return processing, term deposit servicing — each with its own approval hierarchy, branch/role permissions, and dependency on live core-banking data.

What I built

  • Bank Guarantee & Electronic Lending platforms — issuance, amendment, and closure workflows with configurable role/branch routing and compliance PDF generation; the two largest systems in the suite by iteration count.
  • Card Services & Card Payment — full card lifecycle (issuance, activation, block, replacement, PIN change), bulk Excel-based activation, and a batch-workflow engine with failed-job retry.
  • Corporate credit appraisal (MCC) — a purpose-built app modeling 40+ financial risk domains into a multi-stage sanction/approval chain — an Internal Credit Risk Rating engine for corporate lending.
  • SME loan origination — a parallel credit-appraisal workflow with automated executive-summary PDF generation and duplicate-application detection.
  • Limit management, documentation, tax return, and term-deposit/rebate systems — including a dedicated Islamic banking TD workflow and RM-to-branch tagging governance.
  • Shared across all systems: Oracle-view-based core-banking integration, LDAP/AD authentication, Celery/Redis async processing, and — on newer systems — full observability (Prometheus, Loki, Fluentd) plus JMeter load coverage.

Stack

DjangoDRFReactPostgreSQLOracle / SOAPLDAPCelery + RedisDocker ComposePrometheus / LokiJMeter
Outcome — nine-plus mission-critical workflows in production on one shared, actively maintained platform, giving the bank consistent RBAC, audit trail, and core-banking integration across every product.

InteliqX

2024 – Present

Full-stack / Platform Engineer

Multi-Tenant Due-Diligence SaaS for Private Equity

"Revenue Intelligence for Private Equity Due Diligence" — six microservices, taken through a formal security and infrastructure remediation program.

Problem

The platform had grown quickly on individually hand-provisioned EC2 infrastructure with no formal IaC. A security audit surfaced hundreds of findings — including credentials hardcoded for over a year and no structured multi-tenant isolation model — while the product still needed to ship.

What I built

  • Security remediation — removed hardcoded Auth0 and Stripe secrets in favor of environment-based configuration across the auth, billing, and admin-panel services, closing out critical audit findings directly.
  • Multi-tenancy migration planning — authored gap-analysis and migration documentation for moving from ad hoc, user-owned tenancy to an organization-based model backed by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security.
  • CI/CD ownership — maintained AWS CodeBuild pipelines for the Django backend (Docker → ECR) and the React dashboard (S3/CloudFront), iterating configuration as the deployment target evolved.
  • Dashboard visualizations — built early analyst-facing charting (heatmap, treemap, sunburst, waterfall) with Plotly for exploring deal financials.

Stack

Djangodjango-tenantsReactTypeScriptReduxPlotlyFlaskAuth0StripeTerraformAWS
Outcome — hardcoded-secret elimination and CI/CD stabilization delivered directly, with a documented path to full multi-tenant isolation and infrastructure-as-code.

Travelport / SGC Soft OTA

2025 – Present

Backend Engineer

GDS Integration & Vendor API Certification

Replacing a third-party aggregator with a direct GDS integration — and passing Travelport's formal certification process to prove it.

Problem

The agency was reselling flights through a third-party aggregator sitting in front of Travelport. The goal was a direct integration swappable with a one-line config change — no client-side changes — while passing Travelport's multi-round MyTravelport certification before receiving production credentials.

What I built

  • Anti-corruption middleware — a layered Flask service: a hand-built SOAP client for the full booking lifecycle (search → price → fare rules → book → ticket → void → retrieve → cancel), behind a booking-engine abstraction, behind a compatibility facade reproducing the legacy OTA's exact wire contract.
  • Vendor certification — carried the integration through Travelport's multi-round review, resolving reviewer feedback (TraceID continuity, passenger-age consistency, booking-traveler ordering, infant SSR formatting) across submissions, with an automated test-runner covering every mandated scenario.
  • Cross-platform desktop clients — the same booking engine packaged as native-feeling apps for Ubuntu (pywebview) and Windows (PyInstaller).
  • CLI tool — a dual-protocol terminal booking tool (legacy SOAP + newer JSON API) for fast manual testing and diagnostics.
  • Commercial CMS integration — Travelport flight and hotel supplier modules for a multi-supplier PHP travel CMS already integrating Amadeus, Sabre, Duffel, and HotelBeds.

Stack

PythonFlaskSQLAlchemySOAP / XMLTravelport uAPI v52Travelport JSON v11pywebviewPyInstallerPHP
Outcome — a production middleware service, a passed multi-round vendor certification with documented reviewer sign-off, two packaged desktop clients, a CLI tool, and a commercial CMS integration — all on one booking engine.

Wafidflow

2026

Full-stack Engineer

GCC Manpower & Visa Processing SaaS

A multi-tenant platform for recruitment agencies managing the Gulf pre-employment process end to end — visa, medical fitness, embassy, flights.

Problem

Agencies were manually re-typing applicant data from scanned passports and flight tickets — a slow, error-prone step at the front of a multi-stage compliance pipeline (embassy batching, medical clearance, flight booking, contracts).

What I built

  • Serverless OCR pipeline — a passport or ticket photo upload triggers an event-invoked AWS Lambda running docTR, extracting structured fields with confidence scores after a quality gate, then streaming results to the browser over a WebSocket (Django Channels + Redis) in real time.
  • Applicant lifecycle — profile intake, embassy batch grouping, flight booking with its own ticket-OCR auto-fill, and PDF generation for contracts and financial reports.
  • Enterprise auth — JWT plus Google SSO, with a sandboxed architecture isolating tenant data.
  • Analytics dashboard — reports, activity feed, and financial charts, alongside a from-scratch reusable data table (sort/filter/search/pagination/drag-and-drop).
  • Architecture — Django on EC2 behind nginx, React on S3/CloudFront, the OCR engine as a containerized Lambda, Postgres as its own Dockerized service.

Stack

DjangoDRFChannelsCeleryReact 19TypeScriptViteAWS LambdadocTRPostgreSQLRedisDocker
Outcome — an automated, real-time OCR-driven intake flow that removes the slowest manual step in the pipeline, on an architecture that cleanly separates stateful backend, static frontend, and serverless OCR compute.

Children's Gastroliver Center

2025 – Present

Solo Full-stack Developer

Digital Ecosystem for a Pediatric Clinic

Built solo: a real e-prescription app, a schema-driven admin panel, and patient-facing tools — on a lean, serverless foundation.

Problem

A specialist clinic needed to move from paper prescriptions and ad hoc scheduling to a proper digital practice, without the overhead of adopting a full commercial EHR suite.

What I built

  • E-prescription / EHR app — a structured visit workflow (history, examination, diagnosis, investigation, medication) with a searchable medicine database, a dose-combination picker, and a print service rendering a real two-column prescription layout. Packaged as an installable Android app.
  • Schema-driven admin panel — a role-based (Doctor/Nurse) dashboard that introspects the Postgres schema to auto-generate CRUD interfaces for every clinical table, instead of hand-building a screen per table.
  • Public booking micro-site — a lightweight, hCaptcha-protected appointment widget, kept separate from the clinical tools.
  • AI-assisted meal planning — deterministic filtering by allergy and nutrient targets, then an LLM (Groq Llama 3.3 70B, JSON mode) composes a 7-day pediatric meal plan from the curated shortlist — keeping the model grounded.
  • Clinical data pipelines — scrapers feeding the prescription drug database, and a WHO child-growth dataset converted into an interactive growth-chart tool in a patient-facing PWA.

Stack

SupabasePostgresReactIonic / CapacitorPythonSeleniumGroq · Llama 3.3AWS S3 / CloudFront
Outcome — a specialist clinic running on a real, purpose-built digital practice stack — delivered solo on a serverless foundation instead of a costly off-the-shelf EHR.

Frankie4

2024 – Present

Data / Backend Engineer

Data Warehouse Platform

From a custom ETL system to a modern, observable lakehouse-style architecture — without downtime to reporting.

Problem

Business Central, dual-region Shopify stores, and SFTP-delivered reports each spoke a different format on a different schedule; the business needed one reliable, monitored pipeline instead of ad hoc exports.

What I built

  • First-generation platform — custom Python microservices (importer, transformer, scheduler) plus a Flask monitoring dashboard with import history and transformer mapping views, Auth0-based role management.
  • Second-generation rebuild — re-architected onto Airflow (source-specific DAGs), MinIO (raw landing zone), Spark (incremental watermark-based and full-refresh transforms), and dbt (staging → intermediate → marts, with a dedicated data-quality/governance layer).
  • Observability — Grafana dashboards over pipeline performance, storage, and transformation governance, backed by Prometheus.

Stack

PythonFlaskAirflowMinIOSparkdbtPostgreSQLGrafana / PrometheusDocker
Outcome — a production data platform that evolved from a working custom ETL system into a dbt-tested, observable architecture — with no disruption to business reporting during the transition.

Exam-Prep Learning Management System

A mature, multi-year LMS product — and a measured performance-optimization story.

What I built
